Risebird Support

Risebird is an alarm clock that only stops when you complete a wake-up mission.

Get help

  • In the app: Settings → Support opens a chat with the team.
  • Or open an issue: github.com/amanmaqsood/risebird-support/issues

Common questions

  • My alarm didn't ring. Check Settings → Alarm permission is allowed, and run Settings → Ringer check.
  • Cancel a subscription. iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Risebird.
  • Restore a purchase. Settings → Restore purchases in the app.
  • Delete my account. Settings → Delete account. Alarms on your phone stay.
