Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Risebird is an alarm clock. We built it to collect as little as possible.

What stays on your phone

  • Your alarms, missions, sounds and wake history
  • Your answers to the setup questions
  • Camera frames and motion data used by missions. These are processed on the device in real time and are never stored or uploaded.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • Flock (optional). If you join a flock, we store your display name, your flock memberships, your daily check-ins (wake time, rise time, streak) and a push token so friends can nudge you. Sign in with Apple (iPhone) shares only an anonymous user id and, if you allow it, your name.
  • Purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or account details; the app only checks with the store which plan you have.
  • Support chat. Messages you send us, any photos you attach, and basic app details (app version, device model, iOS version, plan, number of alarms, streak) so we can help.
  • Analytics. Risebird does not send usage analytics. There is no advertising identifier and no cross-app tracking.

Your choices

  • Skip Flock and nothing social is stored.
  • Delete your account any time in Settings. This removes your profile, memberships, check-ins, push token and support chats from our servers. Alarms and history on your phone stay.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on the phone.

Contact

Questions: use Settings → Support in the app, or open an issue at https://github.com/amanmaqsood/risebird-support/issues.
