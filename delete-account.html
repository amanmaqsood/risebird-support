Delete your Risebird account

Risebird (by Risebird, on Google Play and the App Store) lets you delete your account and its data at any time.

Delete it in the app

  • Open Risebird and go to Settings → Delete account.
  • Confirm. Deletion happens immediately.

Can't open the app?

  • Open an issue at github.com/amanmaqsood/risebird-support/issues with the display name you used in Flock. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm on the issue.

What is deleted

  • Your Flock profile (display name and account ID)
  • Your flock memberships, wake check-ins and nudges
  • Your push token
  • Your support chat messages and photos

What is kept

  • Alarms and wake history stored only on your phone stay there until you delete the app.
  • Nothing is kept on our servers after deletion. Purchases are managed by Google Play or Apple; deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.
